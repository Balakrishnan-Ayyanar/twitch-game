One rover, a stranded crew, and just enough power to make every choice matter. Chat Mission Control turns a rescue into a shared decision: can a community find the way home before the rover runs out of resources?

Viewers choose north, scan, or supply in seven-second rounds. North opens the northern crossing. Scan opens the central crossing, trading battery for oxygen. Supply opens the southern crossing, restoring battery but costing precious oxygen. The map makes those consequences visible as the rover follows its planned route toward the crew, then the shelter.

Python is the authority. Breadth-first search plans the route, while the backend owns resources, timing and mission outcomes. Each viewer gets one vote per round. Duplicate deliveries cannot become extra votes, and ties and empty rounds have deterministic results. The browser shows the mission; it does not decide which votes count.

The coordinator verified the complete live chain. A real supply command before deployment reached the backend but was correctly marked not running. A fresh command during round one was accepted. That round resolved exactly once: twenty-two battery gained, six seconds of oxygen spent, and the route recalculated. Later rounds had no votes and defaulted north.

The rescue succeeded in twenty-five seconds, carrying the crew to safety with twelve battery and thirty-four oxygen remaining. Subscription acknowledgement, validated notifications and accepted votes are distinct counters, so a connection alone never masquerades as community participation. Twitch access reads chat only: no posting, moderation or broadcast control.

Built and refined in one persistent Codex session, the project also exposed a real integration bug: Twitch's nanosecond timestamps needed strict parsing compatible with Python three point nine. The fix gained regression coverage, and the latest full suite passed one hundred tests without skips.

A small command can change the journey. The goal is simple: give chat an understandable choice, show its consequence, and bring the crew home together.
